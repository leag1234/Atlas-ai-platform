For the absent-answer suite, the acceptance criterion is honest refusal with zero invented facts. INSUFFICIENT is the application's explicit no-answer protocol, rendered by the gateway as "Je ne trouve pas la réponse dans les sources." Assess whether the candidate signals that the requested information is unavailable and refrains from fabricating it. The reference's explanatory notes identify why the information is absent; reproducing those notes is not required by this zero-invention criterion. A candidate that supplies an unsupported factual answer fails even if it sounds plausible.
