# M20 — Honest multimodal boundaries and startup

Requirements: MISSION M20, REQ-ENG-004/005/009/011, REQ-FIN-002, REQ-INF-012/013.

The public chat adapter preserves M12's 6 MiB body, four-image, 4 MiB total decoded
upload and pixel ceilings. Before provider schema validation, a PNG/JPEG larger
than 2 MiB may be recompressed to JPEG at the same dimensions after format and
raster validation. The normalized image must meet the unchanged 2 MiB provider
limit. Transparency may be lost. No remote image download is introduced.

Rejections distinguish bytes, images, dimensions and token context. The journal
contains numeric normalization metadata and sanitized error details, never image
bytes. Honest editing responses are available without inference. Generated-image
metadata adds original_request and model to prompt, rewritten_prompt, seed,
width, height and steps.

The gateway owns image-worker health and startup. Serving enables on-demand
startup. Following M13, initial loading has a separate bounded 900-second window,
recorded as startup_seconds; inference retains its original time and money
allowance. This means wall-clock chat delivery can take startup plus 120 seconds.
The gateway reserves startup infrastructure cost separately under the 30 EUR
milestone ceiling, at no more than 2 EUR/h. The launcher owns shutdown.
