# M10 — Large inputs via passage selection

Requirements: REQ-MOD-004, REQ-ENG-004, POC-P6, docs/15 M10.

The runtime splits the extracted web text into passages with stable offsets and
selects the relevant passages for the question across the entire content.
Selection occurs before generation, without invoking paid synthesis.
Long documents use the existing ingestion/retrieval pipeline; the M10 test
also verifies a response where the evidence is at the end of the document.

Internal selection contract: Unicode text, question, positive byte ceiling;
output composed of exact textual passages, start/end offsets, and total number of
passages examined. The sum of selected texts remains under the ceiling; passages
are returned in source order. No text invented by the selection.

web_fetch accepts an optional question (compatibility with existing clients).
The cache retains the full extracted text under the existing transport limit and
selection is recalculated per question. Final URL and consultation date are
preserved; results keep their untrusted envelope. SSRF limits, robots.txt, time,
tools, and costs remain applicable.

AC: relevant evidence after 2000 bytes retained, question change on the same URL,
Unicode, empty input, no match, resolvable offsets, ceiling respected. Integrated test:
real generation recorded then replayed without provider network in CI, correct response
and resolved citation, time <=120 s and cost <=0.05 EUR per request. No success inferred
from the mere presence of text.

make test-large-input produces BRAIN/eval/large-input.json with answered,
context_exceeded, provenance, latency, cost and source checks. The protected
verifier is invoked by make verify-m10 and in the existing CI path.

Plan: pure selection and tests; web integration and cache tests; integrated gate
web/documents and report; Makefile and scripts/test.sh. No protected files,
no new dependencies, no GPU. Contracts precede implementation.
