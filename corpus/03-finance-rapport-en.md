# HELIOS Ltd — Q3 2026 Financial Report

## Executive summary

In the third quarter of 2026, HELIOS Ltd reported revenue of **48.3 million euros**, up
12 percent compared to the same quarter last year. However, gross margin fell by three
percentage points, from 41 percent to 38 percent, driven mainly by rising component costs
and unfavourable currency movements.

## Revenue by segment

The Hardware segment generated 29.1 million euros, a 6 percent increase year on year. The
Software segment grew faster, reaching 14.2 million euros, up 24 percent. The Services
segment contributed 5.0 million euros, roughly flat versus the prior year.

## Costs and margin

Cost of goods sold rose to 30.0 million euros. The decline in gross margin is attributed to
a 9 percent increase in the price of imported semiconductors and to the weakening of the
euro against the US dollar. Operating expenses were held stable at 11.5 million euros through
tight discretionary spending controls.

## Cash position

Cash and cash equivalents stood at **62 million euros** at the end of the quarter, compared
with 55 million euros at the end of Q2. The improvement reflects strong collection of
receivables and the deferral of a planned capital expenditure programme into 2027.

## Outlook

Management maintains its full-year revenue guidance of 190 to 200 million euros but now
expects gross margin for the full year to land near the lower end of the previously
communicated 39 to 41 percent range. A price increase on selected hardware products will
take effect on 1 January 2027 to partially offset component cost inflation.

## Risks

The main risks to the outlook are continued semiconductor price volatility, further euro
weakness, and slower-than-expected adoption of the new software subscription plan launched
in July 2026.
