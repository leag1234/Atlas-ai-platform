# M10 — selection within large sources

REQ-MOD-004, POC-P6, contract `contracts/m10.md` (PR #38).

retains their offsets, URL, and date, then supplies bounded context to the model.
The cache retains the complete source and recalculates selection per question.
Document passages follow the same selection after hybrid retrieval.
The user question is passed through both HTTP/chat entry points.
