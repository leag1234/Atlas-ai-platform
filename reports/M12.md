Chat accepts a question with PNG/JPEG images and routes messages to the sovereign
vision gateway. Open WebUI uses its native upload. Images are validated before
transport (format, bytes, pixels, role), without URL downloading. Reservation occurs
before inference, with EUR 0.05/120 s limits, verified usage, and typed errors. The
local journal contains image metadata, never their base64.
