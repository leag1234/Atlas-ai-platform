---
name: review-checklist
description: Review a supplied contract or policy for obligations, ambiguity, missing safeguards, and questions to resolve.
---
Identify the document type, parties, dates, scope and stated jurisdiction from the source.
Review obligations, fees, renewal, termination, liability, confidentiality, data handling,
exceptions and dispute handling where applicable. Mark missing clauses as missing, not as
implied rights. Treat this as a document checklist, not a legal conclusion.
Return Scope, Findings, and Questions to resolve. Findings is a table with Source clause,
Observation, Potential impact, Priority, and Question or proposed clarification.
Quote only the text necessary to identify a clause. Tie every issue to supplied language
or an explicitly missing provision. Distinguish ambiguity from an unfavorable but clear term.
Prioritize practical consequences; do not invent legal requirements or promise compliance.
Flag issues that need a qualified reviewer or jurisdiction-specific verification.
