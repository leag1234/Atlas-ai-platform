---
name: structured-summary
description: Summarize a long document with key findings, source locations, limitations, and actionable next steps.
---
Map the document's sections first; inspect all supplied sections before claiming full coverage.
For material too large to read in one call, read bounded sections and retain source locations.
Unless the user specifies another format, deliver these sections:
Overview; Key findings; Evidence and source locations; Limitations; Next steps.
Preserve quantities, dates, exceptions and contradictions. Distinguish the author's claims
from your inference. Prioritize findings that change the reader's decision, not repeated
background. Explicitly identify unread or missing sections and avoid a full-document claim
when only excerpts were provided. Each material finding should trace to a section or page;
use descriptive source labels when page numbers are absent. Do not fabricate citations.
